>**The Amazing Spider-Man is the 2012 superhero film reboot starring Andrew Garfield and Emma Stone, directed by Marc Webb. The movie follows Peter Parker as he gains his powers, uncovers secrets about his missing parents, and fights the Lizard. Its name comes from Marvel's flagship comic book series, which started in 1963 and reached its 1,000th issue in 2026.**

# The Maze Runner
<img width="220" height="270" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/37d7c838-92a9-46d4-ac3e-a803c4f1281e" />

>The Maze Runner follows Thomas, a teenage boy who awakens inside a subterranean service elevator with no memory of his past, identity, or how he got there. He emerges into the Glade, a sprawling, self-sustaining patch of grass and forest inhabited by a rudimentary society of roughly 30 to 50 teenage boys who call themselves "Gladers"

**Main Character**

Thomas.
When he first arrives in the Glade, he has no description of his past, identity, or memories, as his mind has been completely wiped by WICKED. Even physically, author James Dashner deliberately gives him a very vague, universal description in the books—noting only that he is a teenage boy around 16 years old with dark hair—allowing readers to easily project themselves onto him.
